Projects

Research on safe control, formal verification, trustworthy AI, and critical infrastructure.

My research connects formal verification, control theory, and artificial intelligence to establish when AI-enabled physical systems satisfy specified requirements. The five projects below develop this program across safety certificates, resilient autonomy, differential-algebraic systems, neural-network verification, and infrastructure monitoring.

Safe AI Control

Learning, exact verification, and stochastic extensions of neural safety certificates.

Resilient Control and Verified Autonomy

Safe control under sensor faults and attacks, cooperative perception, and verified autonomous navigation.

DAE-Aware Safe Control

Verification and synthesis of safety certificates for differential-algebraic systems.

AI Verification and Interpretation

Verification across neural architectures and analysis of complex AI reasoning.

Critical-Infrastructure Monitoring

Spectral graph anomaly detection and field-informed monitoring for water and electric infrastructure.
