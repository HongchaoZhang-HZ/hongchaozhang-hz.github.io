I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the [Institute for Convergent Software Integrated Systems (ICONS)](https://www.isis.vanderbilt.edu/), Vanderbilt University. I work with [Taylor T. Johnson](https://www.taylortjohnson.com/), [Meiyi Ma](https://meiyima.github.io/), and [Ahmad F. Taha](https://engineering.vanderbilt.edu/people/) at the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI), control, formal methods, and cyber-physical systems.

My research develops foundations and methods for trustworthy decision-making in AI and physical systems, with a focus on safety and resilience. I study how to establish when systems meet their requirements and how to guide their behavior in the presence of errors, uncertainty, or disruptions.

## Research

{% include figure.html path="assets/img/research-overview.png" class="research-overview img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" alt="Research map connecting formal verification, control theory, and artificial intelligence with trustworthy autonomy and critical infrastructure." %}

My established work spans three connected areas:

- **Resilient safe physical AI:** neural control barrier functions, exact certificate verification, fault-tolerant control, and safety under sensor faults and attacks.
- **AI verification and interpretation:** formal verification across neural architectures, neuro-symbolic reasoning, and methods for identifying and correcting errors in complex AI behavior.
- **Verified intelligent autonomy:** integrated reasoning, verification, and protective control for autonomous systems operating under uncertainty and adversarial conditions.

My future research develops these foundations in two directions: **Trustworthy AI Autonomy**, which connects sensing evidence to safe and useful robot behavior, and **Critical Infrastructure**, which combines physical constraints, anomaly analysis, and formal verification for electric and water systems.
