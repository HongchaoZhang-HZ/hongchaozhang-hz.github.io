Committed state and layers

Four layers, one direction of authority. The point of the separation is that a reader can always tell what the project claims from what somebody tried.

Implemented The layers, their modules, and the rules below exist in the published package and are exercised by the repository test suite.

The layer boundary

A records + B graph  ->  committed theorem state
C attempt ledger     ->  what was tried, never what holds
verifier plane       ->  scoped evidence about one target
commit automaton     ->  the only boundary that advances a checkpoint

A records and the B theorem graph

TheoremGraph is the single authoritative store, and it is a frozen value. Every extension returns a new graph and revalidates the whole structure, so a refused transition cannot leave a partial write behind. It holds nothing but tuples of frozen records, with no cached lookup index, so lookup cannot disagree with content_hash().

CommittedState holds the graph, the committed evidence, the committed human decisions, and the current checkpoint, and it exposes no public mutation. It also authenticates itself: recomputed_hash derives the canonical hash from its own contents, and the commit boundary compares that with the checkpoint before it does anything else.

The C attempt ledger

The ledger is append-only history, not state. Its rules are narrow on purpose:

  • Every event carries a sequence number, an actor, a causation identifier, a snapshot pin, an event type, and a canonical payload.
  • Replay is a pure function of the events. It yields candidate projections, never committed records.
  • A duplicate causation identifier with identical content is a no-op. The same identifier with different content is refused.
  • A candidate reaches the commit boundary only through the ledger: submit() rebuilds the candidate by deterministic replay and refuses anything the ledger did not record with the same stream, opening sequence, pinned snapshot, evidence set, and transition hash.

Two statuses that never mix

Mathematical verification status and human governance status are separate fields with disjoint vocabularies. A recorded review changes governance only, and a candidate that links no verifier evidence is refused. This is the rule that keeps a decision to proceed from being mistaken for a proof.

Assumptions have kinds, and the kinds behave differently

KindMeaningBehavior
exported An applicability condition that travels with the supported result. Stays visible to anything that uses the result, reported by applicability_conditions.
carried Context the derivation needs. Inherited by dependents, reported by inherited_context.
proof-local Scoped to the derivation that owns it. Never inherited, the only kind that may be discharged, and discharged by a record on its own derivation.

No record ever acquires a global discharged state, and derivation_assumption_profile splits what one derivation owes into the three kinds.

Bindings are checked at both ends

A binding names the scope its source symbols come from and carries the destination signature its replacement terms are written in. The binding must read the formula's own scope, each bound symbol must be declared there with a matching sort, and every symbol a replacement mentions must be declared by the destination signature and sort-check there. Instantiating an assumption preserves its kind and its owning scope, so a binding transforms an applicability condition and never drops one.

A statement may rest on several derivations

The derivation edge relation is kept acyclic, references must resolve, and one statement may carry several derivations at once. One derivation marked invalid does not unestablish a statement that another valid derivation supports. Which proof is which, and what that means for verification, is the subject of proof-support paths.

Two distinct realized scopes cannot share one verifier request, so a derivation citing two premises instantiated by different bindings is refused at admission rather than represented. Nothing is lost silently: the refusal is explicit and names its gate.
