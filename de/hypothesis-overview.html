Purpose and boundary

The sidebar group Hypothesis Test documents the component the repository calls the Hypothesis Work Unit.

Draft design This page describes a draft local design snapshot. It is not ground truth, and it defines one DE work unit rather than the whole Derivation Engine architecture. The runtime that renders the artifacts described here is implemented; the surrounding design is contestable and expected to change.

Purpose

A Hypothesis Work Unit is the smallest independently recoverable unit in which a human and several agents define, investigate, review, and continue work around one Hypothesis. Everything in the unit exists to make that single question survivable: if a terminal closes, a process dies, or a week passes, the work can be picked up again from files rather than from memory or scrollback.

Stable identity comes from an exact ID. A unit is addressed as HY-001, and that identifier is what every role, path, and status pane agrees on.

What one unit owns

The owned boundary of a single work unit is deliberately small:

  • one human-owned Hypothesis definition;
  • one current Handoff;
  • one Human TODO list;
  • zero or more Review Packets;
  • one live interaction workspace while the unit is active;
  • disposable runtime metadata.

The first four are durable and conceptual. The last two are reconstructable: they can be rebuilt without asking the human to remember anything. Durable artifacts shows exactly which files fall on which side of that line.

Non-goals

The design states its non-goals as plainly as its purpose. A Hypothesis Work Unit is not:

  • the final Derivation Engine architecture;
  • a universal scientific method;
  • a solver;
  • automatic approval of theory or evidence;
  • a publication or deployment pipeline;
  • a holder of destructive authority.

The non-goals are the load-bearing part of the boundary. A work unit routes questions, records answers, and keeps state recoverable. It does not decide whether a Hypothesis is true, and it does not act on the human's behalf where a decision is required.

Why the unit is the boundary

Choosing one Hypothesis as the unit of recovery has a practical consequence: recovery never needs to reconstruct a whole project. A unit knows its own ID, its own durable files, and the roles that were attached to it, so state and recovery can be defined as a small, checkable procedure rather than an open-ended repair.
