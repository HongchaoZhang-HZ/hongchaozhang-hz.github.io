Roles and interaction surfaces

A work unit separates who decides, who routes, who asks, and who executes. The separation is what keeps a multi-agent session from quietly deciding the human's question.

Draft design Role definitions belong to the draft Hypothesis Work Unit design snapshot, not to a settled Derivation Engine architecture.

Current capability The published Hypothesis Work Unit runtime and workflow replay use the Codex App as Manager. That is the implemented role mapping this page describes.

Next-stage target The bilingual user guide proposes Codex or Claude as the one Selected Manager, the single Active Manager of a Project, reached through shared Manager Adapters and a durable Handoff. That selection is a next-stage target, not implemented current Hypothesis behavior.

Roles

Roles in a Hypothesis Work Unit
RoleRuntimeResponsibility
Human owner Obsidian and live dialogue Owns the Hypothesis definition, the decisions, and checkbox completion. Nothing else completes human-owned work.
Manager Codex App Discovers, recovers, routes, verifies, and reports. This is the role that knows the exact work-unit ID and reports where things are.
Dialogue Facilitator Codex CLI in Herdr Asks short questions, maintains the Handoff and TODO text, and delegates work rather than performing large changes inside the conversation.
Worker Claude Code in Auto Mode Executes bounded delegated work.
Status observers Deterministic projections Project role-specific state. They report; they do not decide.

Interaction surfaces

Each role reaches the unit through a surface with a narrow job:

  • Codex App manages an exact work-unit ID.
  • Herdr Dialogue handles short questions and corrections.
  • Herdr Worker handles delegated execution.
  • Status panes show projections and links, so a role can orient without reading the whole history.
  • Obsidian holds the definition, the Human TODO, the Handoff, long evidence, and decisions.

Why the split matters

The Dialogue surface is intentionally cheap and reversible: short exchanges, small text edits, and delegation. The Worker surface is where bounded execution happens. Keeping them apart means a long execution never has to interrupt the human's line of thought, and a conversation never accumulates unreviewed changes.

Agents maintain task wording and links. Agents do not silently approve or complete human work. See Durable artifacts for where that ownership line is written down, and the workflow replay for one pass through all four roles.
